# iBuddies adaptation

Read the local Marketing Team guidance, `workflows/` and current brand/channel
copy references before drafting. Resolve project paths from the workspace rather
than assuming a particular user's Desktop.

- During an open festival window use the relevant campaign copy, following
  `references/festival-campaigns.json` and `references/festival-campaign-copy.md`.
- Apply the available ordering-link-check skill to customer-facing copy. Use the
  exact approved branded `/wa/<slug>` ordering link in every channel's text,
  including Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. Do not substitute link-in-bio or
  DM instructions for that link. Do not adopt upstream X/LinkedIn
  link-placement tactics in these channels.
- Apply buffer-image-check before scheduling/publishing. Include actual product
  photographs alongside generated hero art when the brief requests them. Match
  marinated/raw variants and the approved flavour choices.
- Facebook can explain the gathering and preparation payoff; Instagram should
  lead with a visual hook and readable caption; WhatsApp should be easy to skim,
  forward and order from. Use natural Traditional Chinese/Hong Kong wording and
  purposeful food/price/gift emojis consistent with the writing guide.
- Verify set quantities, per-pack versus per-set prices, delivery promise and gift
  conditions together. A client-approved free-delivery offer is not evidence that
  checkout implements it. Record that operational check separately.
- Avoid revising Cin7 bundles or client-owned stock operations unless the user
  explicitly reopens that work. Preparing a kit does not authorize scheduling.
- Translation can be handled by Codex; do not assume Ollama or a local model exists.

Useful food campaign angles: gathering occasion, preparation convenience, choice
of cooking methods, and the concrete offer. Do not invent tenderness, provenance,
grade, health benefits or savings percentages without product evidence.
